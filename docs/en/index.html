About this ranking

Every city is scored 0–10 on 12 dimensions by community consensus; the overall score is a public weighted average — Internet ×1.0 · Community ×1.2 · Cost of Living ×1.0 · Safety ×1.2 · English-friendly ×0.9 · Walkability ×0.7 · Air Quality ×0.7 · Female-friendly ×0.8 · LGBTQ+ ×0.6 · Nightlife ×0.5 · Quietness ×0.6 · Inclusivity ×0.6. Anyone can propose changes: edit the shared spreadsheet, open a GitHub PR, or file an issue. Every change is recorded in git history and reflected on these pages. Cost figures are monthly budget estimates in USD.

FAQ

How is the score computed? Weighted mean of 12 sub-scores (community & safety weigh 1.2); the formula is public and re-computable from the CSV.
How fresh is the data? Every row carries a 📅 last-updated date, auto-derived from git history and refreshed via daily community syncs.
Can I contribute? Yes — the dataset is fully open: GitHub · shared spreadsheet · or browse the Chinese interactive edition.
